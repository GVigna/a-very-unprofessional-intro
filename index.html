A very unprofessional introduction to my professional life — Giulia Vigna, graphic and motion designer

Hi, I'm Giulia, a graphic and motion designer based in Rome, Italy. This interactive site is an alternative to the traditional CV.

2010 — IED Fashion School

I studied Fashion Design at IED in Rome. Summer jobs in Greece and Egypt.

2012 — New York

Working for Suzanne Rae, feminist and sustainable fashion.

London

Fashion jobs by day, bartending by night, travelling to Istanbul, Milan, Copenhagen, Paris and Florence. Also, cycling.

2016 — Back to Italy: graphic design, motion design and art

Co-founded the art collective Vaste Programme with Leonardo Magrelli. Ten years of freelance graphic design and motion design for agencies, artists, brands, festivals and TV, including House of Ronin, Piccolo Ronin, Roncoroni, Pacifico, Arcade, Banchi, Giano Enoteca and Made in Evolve.

2020 — Megazinne

An independent magazine about breasts created with Ilena Ilardo: three issues and over €20,000 raised for charity (LILT Firenze, D.i.Re, AIDOS). Featured in Rolling Stone, ELLE and Il Messaggero.

2026 — Doccia Fredda

A podcast about love, heartbreak and questionable romantic decisions.

2026 — Sapienza University of Rome

Studying for a BA in Art History.

What I care about

Community, collaboration, teaching and workshops, and making creative work more sustainable.

Freelance projects, collaborations, teaching, workshops, collective experiments. Portfolio: giuliavigna.com · Behance
